# Brand Direction

## Positioning

Primary line:

> Dublin's independent home for books, games and curious finds since 1991.

"Since 1991" is provisional and must be confirmed by the owner before launch. Until confirmed, use "Independent in Dublin for over 35 years" or "from Talbot Street" in production copy.

## Brand Character

Bookmart & Gamexchange should feel:

- Established.
- Independent.
- Dublin-specific.
- Knowledgeable but unpretentious.
- Eclectic but navigable.
- Human and practical.
- Equally credible for affordable paperbacks, rare books, retro games, consoles, vinyl and odd collectibles.

## Observed Shop Cues

From public DublinTown shop imagery:

- Black-and-white tiled shopfront creates a memorable street-level identity.
- Window lettering is direct and retail-practical, including buy/sell messaging.
- Shelving appears purple/lilac, full and heavily browsed.
- Handwritten or simple shelf labels reinforce an independent-shop feel.
- Stock is visibly mixed: books, PlayStation games, retro games, film/media and curiosities.
- Wire racks and glass display areas suggest small valuable items and physical browsing.

These cues should influence the website through colour, labels, product photography crops, shelf-like arrangements and editorial language.

## Voice

Use plain, specific language:

- "One copy available."
- "Photographs show the actual item."
- "Online estimates are subject to inspection in the shop."
- "Collect from 73 Talbot Street."
- "Staff-reviewed condition report."

Avoid inflated selling language:

- must-have
- hidden gem
- ultra rare
- incredible
- investment opportunity
- excessive exclamation marks
- unsupported claims

## Visual Direction

- Warm paper background rather than stark white.
- Charcoal text.
- Deep burgundy primary accent.
- Brass/ticket-yellow small details.
- Purple shelf accent sampled from observed shop shelving.
- Occasional green only when helpful for status or if owner confirms it fits the shop.
- Editorial serif for main headings.
- Clean sans-serif for commerce and admin UI.
- Real photography over illustration.
- Shelf, ticket, catalogue and shop-label references used subtly.

## Hero Direction

The homepage hero should use a real photo of the shopfront or a strong interior photo. The shop name should be the dominant first-viewport signal. The image should remain inspectable, with only enough treatment to maintain text legibility.

Required hero copy:

- Bookmart & Gamexchange
- Dublin's independent home for books, games and curious finds
- Browse new arrivals
- Sell or trade with us
- Visit the Talbot Street shop

## Copy Guardrails

Do not invent:

- owner story
- awards
- testimonials
- founding year
- rare-book expertise claims
- authenticity claims
- product provenance
- opening hours
- email address
- shipping promises

Where information is unknown, make it configurable and require owner confirmation.
